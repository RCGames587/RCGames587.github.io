About

Engineer, builder, and aspiring teacher

I like problems that live where circuits meet code, and I like explaining them even more.

Fresno, CA

I'm an electrical engineering student who learns by building. Most of my projects start with a question: how does this sensor actually work, can I make this cheaper, or could I build that myself?

My coursework spans circuit analysis, Laplace methods, semiconductors, statics, and programming. Outside class, that turns into Arduino and Raspberry Pi builds, rhythm-game hardware, low-power environmental sensing, and game systems in Godot.

I'm also working toward teaching. The tutorials on this site are the start of a workshop series for engineering students: short, hands-on sessions that make the jump from equations to working hardware feel approachable.
